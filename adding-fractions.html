📖 How to play
  1. Step 1 — Find the lowest common denominator. What's the smallest number both bottom numbers fit into? Tap "multiples hint" if you need help!
  2. Step 2 — Rewrite both fractions. Give both fractions the new bottom number. (Whatever you multiply the bottom by, multiply the top by too!)
  3. Step 3 — Add or subtract. Combine the top numbers and keep the bottom. Any fraction equal to the right answer counts — it doesn't have to be simplified!
Example: 1/2 + 1/3 → LCD is 6 → 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6 🎉
